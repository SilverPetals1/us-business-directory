About 907 Heating & Plumbing

907 Heating & Plumbing is Anchorage's premier plumbing, hydronic heating, and mechanical contractor. Headquartered at 5655 Gate Keeper Ave in Anchorage, AK 99504, we provide 24/7 round-the-clock emergency solutions designed specifically for Alaska's demanding sub-arctic climate.

When temperatures drop to minus-twenty and your boiler fails at midnight or a supply pipe bursts, every minute counts. Our master technicians arrive in fully-equipped mobile service vehicles capable of handling high-efficiency condensing boilers, cast-iron boiler overhauls, high-capacity pipe thawing, hydro-jetting, and tankless water heaters.
