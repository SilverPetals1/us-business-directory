Company Overview & Capabilities

WA Quality Blasting & Painting Pty Ltd is Western Australia’s premier specialist in professional abrasive blasting, mobile sandblasting, and high-performance industrial protective coatings. Based strategically in Naval Base, WA, the company caters to heavy industry, mining operations, commercial transport fleets, and structural engineering projects across Greater Perth and Western Australia.

Equipped with an on-site NACE Certified Coating Inspector, every job is executed to stringent Australian (AS/NZS) and international AMPP anti-corrosion standards. From surface preparation, garnet blasting, and profile verification to multi-layer epoxy, polyurethane, and zinc primers, WA Quality Blasting & Painting guarantees maximum adhesion, chemical resilience, and harsh environmental protection for mission-critical infrastructure.

Specialized Core Services

Abrasive Blasting & Sandblasting

High-pressure garnet blasting, rust scale stripping, mill scale removal, paint stripping, and surface profile enhancement.

Industrial & Protective Coatings

NACE certified multi-coat anti-corrosion specifications, marine coatings, epoxies, and UV-resistant polyurethanes.

Mining Equipment Painting

Heavy earthmoving machinery, excavator buckets, chassis, drill rigs, screening plants, and harsh-site refurbishment.

Structural Steel & Fabrications

Pipes, storage tanks, beams, architectural framework, transport trailers, and truck bodies treated for maximum longevity.
