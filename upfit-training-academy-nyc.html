About UpFit Training Academy

UpFit Training Academy is New York City’s premier fitness training program, celebrated for its high-energy, science-backed approach to strength, fat loss, and physical transformation. Built on the philosophy that "People Inspire People — If you want to go far, train together", UpFit operates three state-of-the-art facilities across Manhattan and Brooklyn: Midtown (Herald Square), Downtown (Union Square), and Brooklyn (Gowanus).

With a flawless 5.0-star rating from over 400 client reviews, UpFit combines the personalized attention of 1-on-1 coaching with the motivating camaraderie of semi-private training groups (max 4-6 people per coach). Each member undergoes a comprehensive initial movement and mobility assessment to design a bespoke workout plan tailored to their unique physiology, schedule, and goals.
